# 2026-11-11: One-ink ultramarine system

The user asked for a full UI revamp of every acquisition site. This site looked like a generic blue-on-white SaaS template and shared its palette with aistudioth.com, so it was redesigned rather than polished. Copy, routes, metadata and structured data did not change.

## Decision

- **One ink.** Deep ultramarine `#1b2bd0` is the only hue. Type, rules, icons, buttons and photographs are printed in it on white; `band-ink` sections invert to white type on the ink. No black text, no grey surfaces, no second accent, no shadows. The reference is single-colour printed matter (a cyanotype, an airmail form), which says "light, plain, nothing hidden" better than the category's sky-and-gradient reflex.
- **Photographs as prints.** A `print` wrapper (ink ground) holds a `print-img` (white ground, grayscale, `mix-blend-mode: screen`), so black prints as ink and white stays paper. It unifies the generated photographs, the Suite screenshot, partner logos, machine pictures and app marks without new assets. `mask-image` was tried first for the app marks and rejected: the asset host sends no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`, and cross-origin masks need CORS.
- **The disclosure is the hero's second column.** "Document text goes to cloud models, PII redacted by default" sits in an outlined panel with a redaction diagram, at 19px semibold, instead of a grey footnote. The redaction bar is also the knocked-out stroke in the cloud mark and favicon.
- **Athiti** replaces Bai Jamjuree (shared with two sibling sites). One family for both scripts; its modulated stroke reads as ink. Body is weight 500 at 18px because the face runs light.
- **Fewer islands.** ScrollShowcase, the data-handling section, Pricing, Footer, Reveal and the page motion wrapper were framer-motion islands whose animations were already disabled (`initial={false}`). They are static Astro now; only the Navbar keeps framer-motion.
- **Reveals are transform-only.** Nothing waits at opacity 0 for a class.

## Measured contrast (WCAG 2.x)

| Pair | Ratio |
|---|---|
| white on `ink` #1b2bd0 | 9.12 |
| `ink` on `wash` #f1f2fc | 8.18 |
| `ink-2` #3f4dd8 on white | 6.43 |
| `ink-2` on `wash` | 5.77 |
| `on-ink-2` #dbddf7 on `ink` | 6.82 |
| white on `ink-deep` #1522a2 | 11.78 |
| `edge` #7680e3 (control borders) on white | 3.52 |
| `edge` on `wash` | 3.16 |

`line` #cdd0f5 and `on-ink-line` #5f6bde are decorative hairlines and carry no information alone.

## Constraints kept

- The hero screenshot is cropped to the second app row at every width. The first row shows the Mimir Chat tile captioned "Nothing leaves the building", which is Local-edition wording the Cloud line must not borrow.
- The favicon URL keeps its Next.js cache-busting query; the icon files behind it changed.
- Blog article heroes and figures keep their source colours; they are cited third-party material.

## Rejected

- Keeping black type with blue buttons: the look the redesign exists to leave.
- A sky-blue drench: the first reflex for "cloud", and close to aihardwarethailand.com's cobalt.
- A warm yellow drench: distinct, but weak for a product that handles company documents.
